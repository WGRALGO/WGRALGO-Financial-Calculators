# Financial Calculators v2.0.0

Works on phones and tablets, in portrait and landscape. Like earlier versions, it works offline, with no ads, analytics, trackers, or account.

## What's new
- **More accurate math:**
  - Loans, mortgages, refinancing, and car loans now use the standard monthly rate (APR ÷ 12) most U.S. lenders use, so payments match lender quotes.
  - Compound interest and investment returns now grow at exactly the yearly rate you enter (7% means 7%).
  - Debt repayment still uses daily interest, like most credit cards.
- **Bug fixes:**
  - Lease vs Own Car no longer charges one extra lease payment when the first payment is due at signing.
  - Rent vs Own Home's Advanced (NPV) result no longer favors renting by mistake.
  - Payoff dates now show the month of your last payment.
- **Looks like a real app:** black launch screen with the big logo, new launcher icon, solid app bar, a menu that jumps to any of the 9 calculators, and About / Privacy / Credits panels.
- **Back button works:** closes menus and panels, scrolls back up to the top, and asks before exiting.
- **Phones and tablets, portrait and landscape:** the app rotates freely, the start-screen logo is smaller on landscape phones, and wide tables (like the amortization schedule) scroll inside their box instead of stretching the screen.
- The app name under the icon is now **Financial Calculators**, and the APK is named `WGRALGO-FinancialCalculators-v2.0.0.apk`, like every WGRALGO app.
- Still **100% offline**: no internet permission.

## Important: uninstall v1.0.0 first

This release is signed with a new key and has a new app ID (`org.wgralgo.financialcalculators`), so it can't install over v1.0.0. Uninstall the old app first. The app saves nothing, so nothing is lost.
